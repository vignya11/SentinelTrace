What it does

Tamper-evident log

Canonical JSON, SHA-256 record hashes, explicit genesis, append-only database triggers, read-only verification.

Live monitoring

Server-Sent Events from real database activity with automatic reconnect and polling fallback.

Configurable detection

Six transparent rules. Each alert lists its trigger evidence and states that it is an indicator, not proof.

Investigations

Cases, hypotheses, findings, a UTC timeline and cross-event correlation. Original records are never rewritten.

Evidence and custody

JSON, CSV and PDF snapshots with SHA-256 digests and a hash-linked chain of custody.

Least privilege

Admin, investigator and analyst roles enforced on every route. Optional TOTP two-factor sign-in.

Limits you should know

  • A hash chain alone is not tamper-proof. Someone who can rewrite every record and recompute every hash is only caught by an independent checkpoint (HMAC key and external file kept outside the database).
  • Without any checkpoint, deletion of the newest records cannot be detected.
  • SentinelTrace sees only events it records or that an authorized source sends to its ingest API. It does not watch your operating systems on its own.
  • Alerts are leads for investigation. They are not proof of compromise or of a person's intent.

Demo access

A demo environment runs against a separate database filled with clearly labelled synthetic data. Create it with python scripts/seed_demo.py; the sign-in details are printed once in your terminal and are never stored in the repository.
